### Achievements

- Brief one- and two-character dialogue is recovered through bounded 200 ms visual probes and short OCR bursts instead of lowering the global acceptance threshold.
- Full-frame scene scans capture character names, techniques, artifacts and location/title cards as `S2 · Chữ trong cảnh` while normal dialogue remains in S1.
- High-confidence scene text is translated automatically. Low-confidence text remains reviewable and can be accepted from the subtitle editor with an undoable source/translation update.
- Chinese glyphs are concealed inside their detected region while the surrounding black brush panel, decorative border and other source artwork remain intact.
- Vietnamese replacement text follows horizontal or vertical source geometry, auto-fits the panel and uses bundled Playwrite VN for calligraphic cards.
- Scene text is excluded from TTS, D1 timing QA and user-facing dialogue SRT export.
- Bilibili/channel overlays need repeated edge evidence with temporal spread before automatic masking; ambiguous centered dialogue is not treated as a watermark.
- Preview and FFmpeg export share the same geometry, font-fit and concealment descriptor.

### Known Limitations

- Burned-in Chinese glyphs have already replaced source pixels. The tool conceals them inside the detected box but cannot reconstruct hidden original artwork.
- Scene-text detection and calligraphy classification are heuristic. Uncertain candidates remain in review instead of being silently rendered.
- The worst-case scan does more OCR work than a106. Calls are bounded per 30-second window, but real Paddle latency depends on GPU, footage motion and the number of detected transitions.
