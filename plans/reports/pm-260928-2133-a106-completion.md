### Achievements

- New projects use radius 46, larger subtitle-safe padding and **Che kín** by default.
- Cover rendering combines strong blur with a 42% dark veil; preview strength is calibrated to FFmpeg export.
- OCR removes the supplied Bilibili/channel watermark forms, including conservative fuzzy `bilibili` variants.
- Watermark evidence is separated from subtitle evidence and requires recurrence in at least two frames before an automatic mask is created.
- Automatic watermark masks use `ocr_watermark`, are non-editable as manual blur, and are replaced safely on OCR reruns.
- Ordinary dialogue containing `漫剧`, chapter numbers or animal words remains available to the subtitle pipeline.
- Legacy projects retain their prior blur-only appearance unless the user opts into cover mode.
